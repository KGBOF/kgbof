About Us

The Voice of KGB Officers

The Karnataka Grameena Bank Officers Federation (KGBOF) is the recognized representative organization of officers working in Karnataka Grameena Bank — born from the historic amalgamation of Regional Rural Banks in Karnataka, effective 1 May 2025.

Formerly known as KVGBOF (Karnataka Vikas Grameena Bank Officers Federation), we have transformed alongside the bank, uniting officers across Karnataka under a stronger collective identity.

We combine the ideals of trade unionism with professionalism — ensuring fair treatment, career growth, better working conditions, and a powerful collective voice for every officer.

What We Do

Our Mission & Focus Areas

🛡️

Service Conditions

Protection of salary, allowances, leave entitlements, and retirement benefits for all officers.

📈

Promotions

Timely, transparent, and merit-based promotion processes ensuring fair career growth.

🔄

Transfers

Fair, humane, and policy-based transfer processes with compassion for family situations.

⚖️

Disciplinary Cases

Representation and guidance to officers to ensure due process and fair hearings.

⚖️

Five-Day Banking

Advocacy for work-life balance, five-day banking week, and reduced occupational stress.

💻

Technology

Addressing network issues, software challenges, and improving branch infrastructure.

👥

Staffing

Demanding adequate recruitment and manpower planning to ease officer workload.

🎓

Training

Organizing educational sessions and leadership development programs for members.
